About

I am an Economist at the Bank of Italy, where I lead the Economic Research Unit of the Trento branch. My research lies at the intersection of applied microeconomics, financial intermediation, and regional economics, with growing interests in climate & finance and urban economics. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Trento.
